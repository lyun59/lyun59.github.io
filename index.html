The Problem

Most young people know they should invest—but don't. Over 1,000,000 university students in Taiwan face these barriers simultaneously:

Knowledge Gap

No finance background, no accessible starting point. "I just don't know where to start."

Trust Gap

Banks and brokers are seen as conflict-ridden salespeople. Students fear losing money and biased advice.

Action Gap

Reading books takes too long—most quit before even opening their first account.

Market Opportunity

A massive underserved market with a clear expansion path:

1M+ University Students
70% Non-Finance Background
NT$2B+ TAM (Taiwan Education)

Expansion Path: University Students (Phase 1) → Recent Graduates (Phase 2) → Southeast Asia Market (Phase 3).

Sustainable Business Model

Industry-standard, fully compliant commission from partner brokers when users open accounts (Zero cost to users).
