Problem

Most young people know
they should invest — but don't

"I know I should invest.
I just don't know where to start — and I'm scared of losing money."

Over 1,000,000 university students in Taiwan face all three barriers simultaneously — most without any finance background, and almost no trustworthy place to start.

01

Knowledge Gap

No finance background, no accessible starting point. "I just don't know where to begin."

02

Trust Gap

Banks and brokers are seen as conflict-ridden salespeople. Students fear biased advice and losing money.

03

Action Gap

Reading books takes too long — most quit before they even open their first account.

Solution

A two-layer guidance system
built on trust

From your personal profile to your first brokerage account — every step backed by a structure free of conflict of interest.

Key Differentiator: Zero Conflict of Interest

Our independent advisors are not affiliated with any broker. Their income comes solely from our platform — not from trades — ensuring every recommendation you receive is genuinely neutral.
